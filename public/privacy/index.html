Privacy policy

How Pulsemint Limited handles personal information, written in plain English. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

Pulsemint Limited ("Pulsemint", "we", "us") is a company registered in England and Wales, number 16467783, with its registered office at 104B High Street, Lowestoft, Suffolk NR32 1XW. For questions about this policy or your data, email info@pulsemint.co.uk.

There are two different situations in this policy, and they work differently:

  • Our own visitors and customers. We decide what to do with this information, so we are the data controller.
  • People who ring one of our customers. Our customer decides why those calls are answered and what happens to the details. They are the controller and we handle the calls on their instructions, as their processor. If you rang a business and want your details removed, contact that business. You can also tell us and we'll pass it on.

What we collect, and why

  • Your name, email, phone number and business, when you book a call or get in touch. We use it to reply, set up a pilot and keep a record of what we agreed. Lawful basis: steps before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • Billing details and invoices, for customers. We use them to take payment and keep accounts. Lawful basis: performance of a contract, and our legal obligation to keep records.
  • Recordings and written transcripts of calls the assistant answers. We use them to pass the message on, to check the assistant answered properly, and to improve how it handles calls. Lawful basis: handled for our customer, on their instructions. For our own demonstration calls, our legitimate interest in making the service work.
  • A count of certain clicks on this website, such as "booking link clicked", with the page it happened on. We use it to see which pages are useful. No cookie, no identifier and nothing personal is attached. Lawful basis: legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.
  • Server and security logs, including IP addresses, kept by our hosting provider. They keep the site online and defend against attacks. Lawful basis: legitimate interest in security.

We don't buy marketing lists, and we don't sell or rent anyone's details.

Call recording

Calls answered by the assistant may be recorded and written down. If you are a Pulsemint customer, you are responsible for telling your own callers that calls may be recorded, and we'll help you word it. The assistant tells a caller it's an AI assistant if they ask. If someone asks not to be recorded, tell us and we'll turn recording off for your number.

Who else sees it

We keep the list of suppliers short, and each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Our hosting and network provider, which serves this website and keeps security logs
  • Our voice platform, which connects the calls and produces the recordings and transcripts
  • Our booking tool, which runs the "book a call" page and collects the name and email you type there
  • Our email provider, which carries messages between us
  • Our accountant and payment provider, for invoicing and accounts

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim. Some of these suppliers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by UK approved safeguards such as the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses, or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that go nowhere: up to 12 months, then deleted
  • Customer records: for as long as you're a customer, then up to 6 years, because tax law requires us to keep business records
  • Call recordings and transcripts: up to 90 days by default, unless you ask for a shorter or longer period for your account
  • Website click counts: kept as totals, with nothing that identifies anyone

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete it, where we don't have a reason to keep it
  • Stop or limit how we use it, including objecting to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests
  • Send a copy to you or another provider in a portable format

Email info@pulsemint.co.uk and we'll answer within one month. There's no charge. If you rang one of our customers, we'll pass your request to them, because the records are theirs.

Complaints

If you think we've handled your information badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website doesn't set advertising or analytics cookies. The detail is on our cookie policy page.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters to you as a customer, we'll email you about it.
