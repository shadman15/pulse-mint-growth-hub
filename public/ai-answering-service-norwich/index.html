Why Norwich trades miss so many calls

Work around Norwich is spread out. A plumber or electrician based in the city might be in Taverham in the morning, Wymondham after lunch and out towards the Broads by the end of the day. A lot of that time is spent driving on the A47, the A11 or the A140, which isn't a good time to be answering the phone.

Then there's the housing. The Golden Triangle and the streets off Unthank Road are full of Victorian terraces with original pipework and wiring that's been extended more than once. In the medieval centre around Elm Hill and Tombland there are listed buildings and conservation area rules to work around. Out in the villages, plenty of homes are flint cottages with their own quirks. That makes for jobs that run long, which means calls that go unanswered.

Getting Norfolk addresses right

An answering service is only useful if the address it writes down is the one the customer gave. Norfolk tests that harder than most places.

A caller from Wymondham will say "Windum". Someone in Costessey will say "Cossey". Happisburgh on the coast is closer to "Haze-bruh". A service tuned mainly for American callers will write down nonsense, and you end up ringing the customer back to ask where they live, which isn't a good start.

I set Pulsemint up with the places you actually cover and test it with real Norfolk names before it goes live. It reads the address and postcode back to the caller so mistakes get caught on the call, not when you're sat outside the wrong house.

It helps that NR postcodes cover a huge area, from Norwich itself out to Cromer, Fakenham and even Lowestoft, which is NR32 and NR33. A full postcode tells you straight away whether a job is ten minutes away or an hour.

What matters is whether it gets Norwich addresses right, and whether you can reach a person when you want something changed. You can: you deal with me directly.

Keeping your 01603 number

Your number doesn't change. Whether customers ring a 01603 landline or your mobile, your phone diverts the calls you don't answer to Pulsemint after however many rings you choose. Setup takes about ten minutes of your time. I set the agent up with your business name, the trades you do, the Norwich areas you cover and anywhere you won't travel to. If anything ever fails at our end, calls ring your phone as they do now.
