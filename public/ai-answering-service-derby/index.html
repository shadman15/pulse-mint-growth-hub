Why Derby trades miss calls

Derby is an engineering city. Rolls-Royce, Toyota at Burnaston and the train works at Litchurch Lane employ thousands of people, many of them on shifts. A customer who finishes a night shift notices the leak at eight in the morning. Someone on lates rings about a quote at ten at night. Their free time rarely lines up with your working day.

The housing keeps trades busy too. Victorian terraces around Normanton and the centre. 1930s semis across Allestree, Littleover and Mickleover. Large 1980s and 90s estates at Oakwood. Newer homes around Chellaston and the edges of the city. Each era comes with its own jobs, and they all come in while you're already working on one.

Getting Derby addresses right

Callers usually give the area rather than the street: Allestree, Mickleover, Littleover, Chaddesden, Spondon, Alvaston, Darley Abbey. Some ring from villages out towards the Peak District or Belper and still say "Derby". The agent takes the full address and DE postcode and reads it back, so you know whether it's a ten minute drive or forty before you ring.

Calls at odd hours

With so many shift workers, Derby trades get more calls outside normal hours than most. Pulsemint costs the same at 6am or 11pm as at noon. You decide what counts as urgent, and everything else waits for the morning with the details written down. There's a page about how out of hours cover works if that's your main problem.

What a call looks like

Example call, to show how it works

A shift worker in Mickleover rings at 10.40pm. He's just got home and the downstairs sockets have stopped working. The RCD trips every time he resets it. The rest of the house is fine.

URGENT. Downstairs sockets dead, RCD trips on reset, lights and upstairs OK. Fridge freezer on that circuit. Works shifts, home tomorrow after 2pm. Dan, 07700 900516, 25 Example Drive, Mickleover, DE3.

One thing to be upfront about: Pulsemint is run from Lowestoft, not Derby. The agent answers calls remotely, so where it's based makes no difference to the caller. What matters is whether it gets Derby addresses right, and whether you can reach a person when you want something changed. You can: you deal with me directly.

Keeping your 01332 number

Your number doesn't change. Whether customers ring a 01332 landline or your mobile, your phone diverts the calls you don't answer to Pulsemint after however many rings you choose. Your van, your website, your Google Business Profile and your Checkatrade or MyBuilder listing all stay exactly as they are.

Setup takes about ten minutes of your time. I set the agent up with your business name, the trades you do, the Derby areas you cover and anywhere you won't travel to. If anything ever fails at our end, calls ring your phone as they do now.

Is it worth it for a Derby business?

Only your own numbers can answer that. Count last week's missed calls, take out the spam and suppliers, and be realistic about how many were jobs you'd have won. Multiply by what a job is worth to you. If it comes to more than £199 a month, it pays. If it doesn't, a better voicemail and missed call text back may be all you need.
