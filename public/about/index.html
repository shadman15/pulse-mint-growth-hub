About Pulsemint: the one-man answering service for UK trades

Pulsemint answers the calls you can't get to, writes down what the caller wants, and texts you the details within a minute. It's built and run by one person in Lowestoft, and you deal with him directly.

Why it exists

Most small firms lose work the same way. The phone rings while you're under a sink, up a ladder or driving. It goes to voicemail. The caller hangs up and rings the next name on the list. Nobody finds out what that costs, because the job never shows up anywhere.

Hiring someone to answer the phone costs more than most sole traders and small teams can justify, and a call centre charges by the minute and reads from a script. Pulsemint sits in between: an assistant that picks up every time, takes proper details and passes them straight to you, for a flat monthly price.

What it does

  • Picks up calls you don't answer, at any hour, including weekends
  • Answers in your business name and asks what the caller needs
  • Takes the caller's name, number, the job and the address
  • Texts you a short summary within about a minute, so you can ring back
  • Tells callers it's an AI assistant if they ask
  • Doesn't quote prices or promise dates, because those are yours to give

Who runs it

I'm Shadman Chowdhury. I build and run Pulsemint myself from Lowestoft in Suffolk. When you start a pilot, I set the assistant up for your business, listen to how it handles your first calls and change the wording until it sounds right. There's no support queue and no account manager. If something's wrong, you tell me and I fix it.

What it costs

£199 a month, including up to 50 captured calls. No contract and you can stop whenever you like. Before that there's a free 14-day pilot, which takes about ten minutes of your time to set up. If it doesn't catch jobs you would otherwise have lost, don't keep it.

The full terms are on the terms of service page, and what happens to call data is set out in the privacy policy.
