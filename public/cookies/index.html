Pulsemint/Cookie policy

Cookie policy

The short version: this website doesn't set any advertising or analytics cookies, so there's no cookie banner to click away. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we do instead of tracking

We count a small number of actions, such as someone clicking through to the booking page, along with the page it happened on. That count is sent without a cookie, without an identifier and without anything that could point back to you. We can see that a page sent ten people to the booking link. We can't see who they were, or follow them anywhere else.

Cookies this site sets

CookiePurpose
NonePulsemint sets no cookies of its own on this website.

Other services, when you choose to use them

Two parts of the site hand you over to another company, and those companies set their own cookies under their own policies:

  • The booking page. Clicking "book a call" takes you to our booking provider's own site to pick a time.
  • The test call. If you choose to hear the assistant answer, your browser connects to our voice platform, and asks your permission for the microphone first. Nothing is recorded until you start the call, and you can end it at any time.

Your browser may also store small amounts of data needed to make a page work, such as keeping a connection alive. These are strictly necessary and don't need your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Because we don't rely on them, nothing on this site will break if you do.
