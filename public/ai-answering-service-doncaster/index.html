A city made of villages

Doncaster became a city in 2022, but most of the borough doesn't feel like one. Around the town centre there's the Minster, Frenchgate and the racecourse where the St Leger is run. Beyond that are the old pit villages: Armthorpe, Edlington, Bentley, Rossington, Thorne, Stainforth, Askern, Conisbrough, Mexborough. Each has its own identity, and callers will name the village rather than say Doncaster.

That spread means a lot of driving between jobs, often on the M18 and A1(M), and a lot of calls you can't take while you're at the wheel.

The housing

Many homes in the former mining villages were built for miners and their families, as terraces and semis of a similar age. They tend to need the same work at the same time: heating systems past their best, old wiring, roofs and chimneys that need attention. Add newer estates on the edges of the borough, and a growing number of homes for people working at the iPort and the other big warehouses, and there's steady work for local trades.

Getting Doncaster addresses right

The agent takes the village or area, the full address and the DN postcode, and reads it back to the caller. It matters here because Doncaster's DN postcodes run a long way out. Some of the places you're asked to go are a fair drive, and you want to know that before you ring back, not after you've agreed to go.

If you only cover part of the borough, I'll set the agent up to tell callers outside your area that you don't travel that far.
