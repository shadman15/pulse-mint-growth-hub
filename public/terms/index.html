These terms apply when you start a pilot or a paid subscription. If you're agreeing on behalf of a business, you confirm you're allowed to.

2. What the service does

Pulsemint answers calls you don't pick up, asks the caller for their name, number, the job and the address, and sends you a summary by text, usually within about a minute. The assistant answers in your business name, confirms it's an AI assistant if a caller asks, and doesn't quote prices or promise appointment dates unless you've asked us to set it up that way.

It isn't an emergency service. It can't dispatch anyone, and it shouldn't be used as the contact point for anything where a delay would be dangerous.

3. The free pilot

The pilot runs for 14 days from the day your number starts forwarding to us. There's nothing to pay and no card needed to start it. At the end, you either start a paid subscription or we switch the number back and delete the pilot data. We may end a pilot early if it's being used in a way these terms don't allow.

4. Price and payment

  • £199 a month, including up to 50 captured calls in a month. A captured call is one the assistant answers and sends you a summary for.
  • Payment is monthly in advance, from the date the paid subscription starts.
  • If you go over 50 captured calls, we'll contact you before charging anything extra and agree a price with you first. We never add charges you haven't agreed.
  • Prices are in pounds sterling. If we become VAT registered we'll tell you, and VAT will be added at the rate in force.
  • We may change the price, but not during a month you've already paid for, and not without at least 30 days' notice by email.

5. Cancelling

There's no contract term and no cancellation fee. Email us and we'll stop the service at the end of the month you've paid for. We don't refund part months, except where the law says we must or where we've got something wrong. You can point your number back at any time, and we'll help you do it.

6. What we need from you

  • Set up call forwarding from your number, or use a number we provide, as agreed
  • Tell your callers that calls may be recorded. We'll give you suitable wording
  • Give us accurate information about your business for the assistant to use, and tell us when it changes
  • Keep your own copies of anything you need. The summaries we send are yours to keep
  • Check the messages we send and ring customers back yourself. We take the details; the work is still yours

7. What you must not use it for

You must not use Pulsemint to break the law, to make marketing calls to people who haven't agreed to receive them, to handle emergency or safety-critical calls, or in any way that could harm the service or other users. We can suspend or end the service if that happens, and we'll tell you why.

We handle call data on your instructions, keep it secure, use suppliers bound to the same standards, and help you answer requests from your callers. The detail is in our privacy policy, which forms part of these terms. You remain responsible for telling your own callers how their information is used.
